Day-Wise Itinerary

0

Day 0: Boarding Bus from Delhi

Gather & Report at Kashmiri Gate Metro Station (Delhi) at 8:30 pm and meet our trip captain. Depart for Shimla (Overnight journey). Pit stop for dinner.

Overnight journey
1

Day 1: Reach Shimla | Kinnaur gate | Sangla/Chitkul

Early morning departure. Drive through the beautiful Sutlej Valley. Enter the scenic Kinnaur region. Cross Rampur, Jeori and Karcham. Reach Sangla by evening. Explore Sangla market if time permits.

Stay: Sangla/Chitkul
2

Day 2: Chitkul | Suicide Point | Kalpa

Breakfast and departure for Chitkul, the last inhabited village near the Indo-Tibetan border. Explore Chitkul and the Baspa Valley. Continue towards Kalpa. Visit Suicide point and enjoy views of the Kinner Kailash range.

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner | Stay: Kalpa
3

Day 3: Khab Sangam | Nako lake | Gue monastery

Early breakfast. Begin the spectacular drive towards Spiti. Cross Khab sangam and enter the Spiti Valley. Stop at Nako Lake & Nako Village. Visit Gue Monastery, 550 years old mummy. Evening at leisure.

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner | Stay: Nako/Tabo
4

Day 4: Tabo monastery | Dhanker monastery | Kaza

Breakfast and departure. Visit Tabo Monastery & Dhanker monastery. Continue towards Kaza. Enroute explore the traditional villages of Spiti. Reach Kaza and check in. Evening free to explore Kaza market.

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner | Stay: Kaza
5

Day 5: Kaza Local Sightseeing

After Breakfast and explore the famous high altitude villages: Key Monastery, Hikkim - one of the world’s highest post office, Komic – one of the highest motorable villages, Langza – famous buddha statue and fossil rich landscape. Evening free to explore Kaza market.

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner | Stay: Kaza
6

Day 6: Chicham Bridge | Kunzum pass | Chandrataal

After Early morning breakfast, explore Asia’s highest suspension bridge Chicham bridge. Drive through the rugged Spiti landscape. Cross Kunzum Pass subject to road/weather conditions. Continue towards Chandratal Lake. Trek/walk to the lake from the parking area. Enjoy the spectacular Himalayan surroundings. Night under the stars at Chandratal.

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner | Stay: Chandrataal
7

Day 7: Chacha Chachi Dhaba | Depart for Delhi

Wake up early & witness the beautiful sunrise. Have breakfast & Check out. Visit Chacha Chachi Dhaba for tea break. Overnight drive to Delhi & reach Delhi next day early morning with Loads of Memories.

Meals: Breakfast | Trip Ends
