The Journey At A Glance

The Kuari Pass with Pangarchulla Peak Trek is one of the most complete and thrilling Himalayan adventures in Garhwal. Tracing Lord Curzon's historic mountain passage, the route rewards trekkers with unprecedented front-row vistas of India's grandest peaks: Nanda Devi (7,816 m), Kamet (7,756 m), Dronagiri, Trishul, and Chaukhamba. Adding the summit push of Pangarchulla Peak (15,069 ft) provides the ultimate adrenaline rush of traversing snowy knife-edge ridges and rugged boulder fields.

Day-Wise Itinerary (6D / 5N)

1

DAY 01: Rishikesh to Karchi

Drive: Approx. 255 km | 8–9 hours | Base Altitude: Approx. 7,800 ft

  • Departure from Rishikesh promptly at 7:00 AM.
  • Drive to Karchi via picturesque mountain routes passing the sacred river confluences (Devprayag, Srinagar, Rudraprayag, Karnaprayag). Breakfast and lunch en route (self-paid).
  • Ascend towards the serene Garhwali village of Karchi nestled on the slopes overlooking the Dhauli Ganga valley.
  • Check-in at Karchi Guest House / Wooden Homestay / Hotel.
  • Delicious dinner, team introduction, and briefing by your qualified Trek Leader.
Meals: Dinner | Stay: Wooden Homestay / Hotel in Karchi
2

DAY 02: Karchi to Khullara Campsite

Trek: Approx. 5 km | 4–5 hours | Altitude: 11,100 ft

  • Head out to Karchi village after breakfast to commence the trekking journey.
  • Trek up through rich forests of towering chestnut, oak, and rhododendron trees carpeted with wild flora and alpine streams.
  • Enjoy a healthy packed lunch en route amidst tranquil mountain clearings.
  • Arrive at the spectacular Khullara Campsite, located on an open high meadow offering panoramic views.
  • Acclimatization walk in the evening to prepare your body for altitude.
  • Evening tea, snacks, hot vegetarian dinner, and overnight stay in alpine tents under the starry skies.
Meals: Breakfast, Packed Lunch, Evening Snacks & Dinner | Stay: Khullara Campsite (Alpine Tents)
3

DAY 03: Khullara to Kuari Pass and Back to Khullara

Trek: Approx. 7–8 km | 5–6 hours | Pass Altitude: 12,516 ft

  • Breakfast at Khullara campsite facing the dawn glow on snow-capped peaks.
  • Trek to the legendary Kuari Pass along the historic Lord Curzon trail.
  • Standing at Kuari Pass (12,516 ft) unveils an unmatched panoramic spectacle of Nanda Devi, Kamet, Dronagiri, Hathi-Ghodi Parvat, Trishul, Neelkanth, and Chaukhamba.
  • Spend cherished moments taking photographs and soaking in the grandeur of the Greater Himalayas.
  • Head back to Khullara campsite for a warm, freshly prepared lunch.
  • Acclimatization walk around the campsite, gear check (microspikes, gaiters, headlamps) and rest for tomorrow's big summit push.
  • Evening snacks, dinner, and early bedtime.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks & Dinner | Stay: Khullara Campsite
4

DAY 04: Khullara to Pangarchulla Peak Summit & Return

Summit Day | Trek: Approx. 10–12 km | 8–10 hours | Summit Altitude: 15,069 ft

  • Summit day! Early alpine start to Pangarchulla Peak after a hearty breakfast (departure around 3:30–4:00 AM with headlamps).
  • Navigate through snowy ridges, challenging boulder fields, and steep inclines as the morning sun paints Nanda Devi in brilliant gold.
  • Reach the coveted summit of Pangarchulla Peak (15,069 ft) and experience an exhilarating 360-degree top-of-the-world view.
  • Celebrate your achievement, capture summit photographs, and absorb the raw majesty of the Himalayas.
  • Carefully descend and return to Khullara campsite in the afternoon.
  • Rest, hot tea, snacks, celebratory dinner, and deep rest at camp.
Meals: Breakfast, Packed/Hot Lunch, Evening Snacks & Dinner | Stay: Khullara Campsite
5

DAY 05: Khullara to Karchi Village

Trek: Approx. 5 km | 3–4 hours descent

  • Wake up to a peaceful morning and enjoy breakfast with your fellow trekkers.
  • Start descent towards Karchi along the forested mountain trail.
  • Arrive in Karchi village and check in to your wooden homestay / hotel.
  • Take a hot shower, rest, and enjoy a relaxed evening.
  • Embark on a traditional village tour to interact with welcoming Garhwali locals and learn about Himalayan village life.
  • Delicious local dinner and comfortable overnight stay in hotel.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner | Stay: Wooden Homestay / Hotel in Karchi
6

DAY 06: Karchi to Rishikesh

Drive: Approx. 255 km | 8–9 hours

  • Depart from Karchi for Rishikesh after breakfast.
  • Drive back through the scenic Alaknanda river valley and Garhwal mountains.
  • Expected arrival in Rishikesh by 5:00 PM.
  • The memorable journey concludes with fond farewells and lifelong friendships.
Meals: Breakfast | Drop: Rishikesh / Haridwar

The Kuari Pass with Pangarchulla Peak Trek is widely considered one of the finest classic high-altitude trails in the Garhwal Himalayas. Originally popularized in 1905 when Lord Curzon crossed the pass, it provides an amphitheater view of India's most celebrated peaks, including Nanda Devi, Kamet, Dronagiri, and Trishul.

The Dual Thrill: Pass Crossing + Peak Summit

This itinerary combines the scenic alpine beauty of Kuari Pass (12,516 ft) with the technical thrill of summiting Pangarchulla Peak (15,069 ft). The summit day presents a true mountaineering challenge involving early morning climbs through steep snow gullies, boulder fields, and ridgelines.

Best Season for Kuari Pass & Pangarchulla

Spring (March to May): Deep snow covers the higher ridges, making it ideal for snow climbing with microspikes and crampons, while the lower forests bloom with vibrant red and pink rhododendrons.
Autumn/Early Winter (October to December): Crisp blue skies, sharp visibility, and fresh dusting of snow on the high peaks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is previous trekking experience required?

While beginners with high physical fitness can complete Kuari Pass, Pangarchulla Peak summit requires endurance, mental grit, and good footwork across rocky and snowy terrain. Regular stamina training is highly advised.

What peaks will I see from Kuari Pass and Pangarchulla?

You will see breathtaking close-up vistas of Nanda Devi (India's second highest mountain), Kamet, Dronagiri, Hathi-Ghodi Parvat, Trishul, Chaukhamba, Bethartoli, and Neelkanth.

What happens if weather conditions prevent the Pangarchulla summit?

Safety is always our foremost priority. If adverse weather, heavy snow, or high winds prevent a safe summit push, the trek leader will guide the team to explore the breathtaking viewpoints around Kuari Pass and Khullara ridge safely.
