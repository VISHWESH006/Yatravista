Day-Wise Itinerary

0

Day 0: Departure from Delhi

Reporting: 9:30–10:30 PM at Akshardham Metro Station, Delhi. Overnight travel with music & games onboard Tea/Dinner halt en route (self-paid).

Overnight journey to Rishikesh
1

Day 1: Rishikesh to Sari Village Via Srinagar

Early arrival at Rishikesh. Visit Devprayag Sangam & Dhari Devi Temple. Check-in at Sari. In the evening, explore the village, enjoy a bonfire & group activities. Overnight in homestay/campsite.

Meals: Dinner | Stay: Homestay / Campsite
2

Day 2: Tungnath & Chandrashila Trek

Drive to Chopta base (via Baniyakund). Darshan at Tungnath Temple, the highest Shiva temple in the world. Climb to Chandrashila summit for a 360° Himalayan view. Return to base and head to Sari for dinner and rest.

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner | Stay: Homestay / Campsite
3

Day 3: Deoriatal Lake Trek & Departure

After breakfast, check out from the hotel. Morning trek to Deoriatal (Altitude: 7,999 ft). Free time by the lake for reflection & photography. Return by noon and depart for Delhi. Optional: Omkareshwar Temple visit. Reach Rishikesh by Evening and continue overnight journey to Delhi.

Meals: Breakfast
