The Journey At A Glance

Set out from Dehradun for a classic five-day Himalayan winter trek to Kedarkantha. The route climbs through enchanting pine, oak and maple forest trails inside Govind National Park, passing the iconic frozen lake of Juda Ka Talab and pristine snow-clad alpine camps before reaching the 12,500 ft summit with panoramic 360-degree views of Swargarohini, Black Peak, and Bandarpoonch.

Starting Point Sankri Village (6,400 ft)
Region Uttarkashi, Uttarakhand
Total Trek Distance Approx. 20 km
Max Altitude Approx. 12,500 ft (3,810 m)
Grade Easy to Moderate
Best Time October to April

Day-Wise Itinerary

1

Day 1: Dehradun to Sankri Village

Drive: Approx. 200 km | 7–8 hours | Altitude: 6,400 ft

  • Early morning pick-up from Dehradun (ISBT / Railway Station). Meet your fellow trekkers and trek leader.
  • Enjoy a picturesque drive via Mussoorie, Nainbagh, Damta, Purola and Mori, alongside the crystal waters of Yamuna and Tons rivers.
  • Enter Govind Wildlife Sanctuary / National Park and arrive at Sankri, a picturesque wooden Himalayan village.
  • Check-in at your homestay/hotel, rest after the scenic journey, explore village market, and attend the evening briefing by your trek leader.
Meals: Dinner | Stay: Homestay / Hotel in Sankri
2

Day 2: Sankri to Juda Ka Talab or Hargaon

Trek: Approx. 5 km | 4–5 hours | Altitude: 9,100 ft

  • Begin your trek after a warm breakfast, following the gentle trail that winds out of Sankri village.
  • Trek through dense forests of tall pines, silver oak, and rhododendron trees with crisp Himalayan mountain air.
  • Cross quaint wooden bridges, listening to gushing mountain streams and songbirds.
  • Reach Juda Ka Talab, a famous frozen winter lake campsite nestled between towering pine trees (or Hargaon meadow campsite depending on conditions).
  • Enjoy a hot lunch, take an acclimatization walk around camp, followed by evening tea, snacks, dinner, and stargazing in alpine tents.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks & Dinner | Stay: Alpine Tents
3

Day 3: Reach Kedarkantha Basecamp

Trek: Approx. 3 km | 5–6 hours | Altitude: 11,250 ft

  • Continue towards Kedarkantha Basecamp along snow-laden trails through thinning pine tree groves.
  • The trail opens up into vast alpine meadows (Bugyals) offering breathtaking views of Bandarpoonch, Swargarohini, Black Peak (Kalanag), and Ranglana peaks.
  • Arrive at basecamp and set up camp under the shadow of the conical Kedarkantha peak.
  • Use the afternoon to acclimatise, fit microspikes and gaiters, explore the snowy surroundings, and receive instructions for the dawn summit attempt.
  • Early dinner and rest to prepare for tomorrow's summit push.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks & Dinner | Stay: Alpine Tents
4

Day 4: Summit Push & Descent to Juda Ka Talab / Hargaon

Trek: Approx. 9 km | 7–8 hours | Summit Altitude: 12,500 ft

  • Start before dawn (around 3:30–4:00 AM) with headlamps on for the final summit ascent.
  • Climb the steep rocky terrain and crisp snow-covered ridges under a star-studded sky.
  • Reach Kedarkantha Summit (12,500 ft) just in time for an unforgettable sunrise over the Garhwal Himalayas.
  • Marvel at a 360-degree vista of peaks: Swargarohini, Bandarpoonch, Black Peak, Gangotri, and Kinnaur ranges. Pay respects at the summit Shiva-Ganesha stone shrine.
  • Descend back to basecamp for warm rest and lunch, then continue descent to Juda Ka Talab or Hargaon campsite.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks & Dinner | Stay: Alpine Tents
5

Day 5: Trek back to Sankri & Drive to Dehradun

Trek: Approx. 5 km | Drive: Approx. 198 km | 6–7 hours

  • After a warm breakfast, descend through the familiar pine forests towards Sankri.
  • Reach Sankri by late morning, freshen up and board the waiting vehicle for Dehradun.
  • Enjoy the scenic return drive alongside Garhwal rivers and hills.
  • The trek concludes on arrival at Dehradun Railway Station / ISBT by 6:00–7:00 PM with unforgettable Himalayan memories.
Meals: Breakfast

The Kedarkantha Trek is indisputably the crown jewel of winter trekking in the Indian Himalayas. Rising to an impressive altitude of 12,500 feet inside Govind National Park in the Uttarkashi district of Uttarakhand, this trek offers a complete Himalayan adventure wrapped into a beginner-friendly package.

Why Kedarkantha is the Queen of Winter Treks

Unlike many summits that require technical mountaineering skills, Kedarkantha provides an authentic summit experience accessible to both beginners and seasoned hikers. As you climb from the picturesque base village of Sankri, you walk through dense groves of pine and oak, beside the legendary winter frozen lake of Juda Ka Talab, and emerge into sprawling snow-covered alpine meadows.

The 360-Degree Summit Panorama

Reaching the summit at dawn rewards trekkers with one of the grandest panoramas in Uttarakhand. You are greeted by clear views of the Swargarohini massif (I, II, and III), Black Peak (Kalanag), Bandarpoonch, Har Ki Dun valley, and the distant peaks of Kinnaur and Gangotri.

Best Season to Trek

Winter (December to April): The best time for snow enthusiasts. Trails and pine trees are draped in thick fresh white snow, and Juda Ka Talab freezes solid.
Autumn (October to November): Crisp mountain weather, crystal-clear skies, and vivid golden-brown forest foliage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Kedarkantha suitable for first-timers and beginners?

Yes! Kedarkantha is one of the most accessible summit treks in India. Basic cardiovascular fitness (such as brisk walking or jogging 4-5 km in 30 minutes) is sufficient preparation.

What is the temperature like on the trek?

In winter (Dec–Feb), daytime temperatures range from 5°C to 12°C, while nighttime temperatures at basecamp can drop between -2°C and -10°C. Quality multi-layer clothing and sub-zero sleeping bags (provided by Yatra Vista) ensure comfortable warmth.

How is mobile network coverage?

BSNL and Airtel signals are available intermittently up to Sankri village. Beyond Sankri on the trail, mobile network connectivity is not available, allowing you to completely disconnect in nature.
