Day-Wise Itinerary

0

Day 0: Departure from Delhi

Assemble at Akshardham Metro Station between 9:30 PM – 10:30 PM. Meet your Trip Captain and fellow travelers. Board the vehicle and begin your journey towards Udaipur. Enjoy fun introductions, music, and group bonding activities. Halt at a roadside dhaba for refreshments and dinner (self-paid).

Overnight journey to Udaipur
1

Day 1: Royal Udaipur Welcome

Arrive in Udaipur and check in to the hotel. Visit the beautiful Sahelyon Ki Bari, known for its lush gardens and fountains. Explore the scenic Fateh Sagar Lake and enjoy lakeside views. Visit the Aquarium and the iconic Maharana Pratap Memorial. Spend the evening at Fateh Sagar Market, enjoying local street food and vibrant surroundings. Return to the hotel for dinner and a musical evening.

Meals: Dinner | Stay: Hotel in Udaipur
2

Day 2: Heritage & Culture of Udaipur

Enjoy breakfast and head out for a full-day sightseeing tour. Visit the majestic City Palace, a symbol of Rajput grandeur. Enjoy a boat ride on the stunning Lake Pichola. Seek blessings at the historic Jagdish Temple. Take the Karni Mata Ropeway for panoramic views of the city. Explore the picturesque Gangaur Ghat and attend a folk performance at Bagore Ki Haveli or explore local markets.

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner | Stay: Hotel in Udaipur
3

Day 3: Mount Abu Escape & Departure

Enjoy breakfast and check out from the hotel. Proceed towards Mount Abu (Approx. 165 km drive). Visit the famous Nakki Lake and Toad Rock. Stroll through the lively Mall Road. Visit the renowned Dilwara Temples, famous for their intricate marble architecture. Spend some leisure time exploring the hill station. Begin your return journey towards Delhi in the evening.

Meals: Breakfast
